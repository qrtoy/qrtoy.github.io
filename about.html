qrtoy

Apps stored in QR codes, almost completely offline.

How it works

Put a data URL after the # and it gets loaded into a sandboxed iframe. The fragment is never sent to a server, so the whole app lives in the link itself.

https://qrtoy.github.io#data:text/html,<div>hello world</div>

Why almost offline?

Browsers block top-level navigation to data URLs, so a QR code can't open one directly. It needs a real URL to bootstrap from, which requires internet the first time. After that a service worker serves from the cache. Once you've visited one qrtoy, you can visit any other without internet until the cache expires (7 days unused in Safari) or is cleared.
