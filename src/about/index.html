qrtoy

apps stored in QR codes, almost completely offline

Try it out

How it works

Put a data URL after the # and it gets loaded into an iframe.

https://qrtoy.github.io#data:text/html,<div>hello world</div>

Why almost offline?

QR codes can't open data URLs directly. qrcode.github.io is a tiny website that loads data URLs and stays cached for offline use. Once you've visited one qrtoy, you can visit any other without internet until the cache expires. Expiring is uncommon, unless you manually clear it or use Safari.
